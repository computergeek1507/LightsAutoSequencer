Words & vocals

Separates the singing from the music, writes down the words with their timing, and tracks the melody. Everything runs on this computer. The first run downloads about 400 MB of models, which your browser keeps; after that a song takes a minute or two.

Fixing timing: in the timeline's Words row, drag a word's left edge to move where it starts, or drag the word to move it. Drag a line in the Lyrics row to move the whole line. Starts snap to where the voice comes in (hold Alt to place freely). Click a word, then use ← → to nudge it 10 ms (Shift: 50 ms). Ctrl+Z undoes.

Export timing

The .xtiming file imports straight into xLights: right-click a timing track header, then Import Timing Tracks.

Your xLights show

Which folder does xLights use as your show folder? It is the one holding xlights_rgbeffects.xml and xlights_networks.xml (often C:\xLights\Show). The page only reads it; nothing is changed unless you save a sequence into it.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser. Your song and your show folder are never uploaded. The first time you find the words, the voice and speech models (about 400 MB) download once from Hugging Face and are kept by your browser.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can open your show folder and run the models on the graphics card). DMX fixtures and image models show as single dots in the preview (the saved sequence is fine). Ideas start from one person's sequencing style; Feeling, Prop mix and Prop types steer them. New here?
