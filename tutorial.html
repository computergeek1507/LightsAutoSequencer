Lights Auto Sequencer how to use it

💬 Discord

Lights Auto Sequencer turns a song into an xLights sequence. It finds the beats, the parts of the song and the words, suggests which props light up and how, lets you watch it on a picture of your house, and saves a .xsq file that xLights opens and renders like any other sequence. Everything runs in your browser: your song and your show are never uploaded.

Open the app with the guided tour Just open the app 💬 Join the Discord

Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. They can open your show folder and run the voice and speech models on your graphics card. Firefox and Safari work, but more slowly and with fewer options; phones are too small.
  • Have xLights set up already. The app reads your layout (models, groups, house photo) from your xLights show folder: the folder that holds xlights_rgbeffects.xml and xlights_networks.xml.
  • An MP3 of the song. Any song file your browser can play works.

Part 1: the song

  1. Load a song

    Drop an MP3 onto the page, or press choose a file. In a few seconds the app works out the tempo, the beats and bars, the loud and quiet parts, and the drum hits.

  2. Check the beat

    Press Play and tick Beat click: you hear a click on every beat, a higher one on the first beat of each bar. If the clicks drift or sit between the drums, fix it in the Tempo grid panel (½ tempo or 2× tempo, Tap tempo along with the song, or change which beat starts a bar). Everything else is built on this grid, so it is worth a minute.

    Press Space to play or pause and Home to go back to the start.

  3. Check the song's parts

    The app splits the song into parts (verse, chorus, ...). Its names are guesses: in the Sections panel, rename parts so they match what you hear, split a part where the music changes, join two parts, or drag a boundary in the timeline. Each part gets its own lights later, and parts with the same name ("Chorus 1", "Chorus 2") can share ideas.

  4. Find the words (optional)

    For singing faces and words on a matrix, go to Words & vocals and press Find words & vocals. Paste the lyrics first if you have them: the timing comes out better. The first time, the browser downloads the voice and speech models (about 400 MB, kept for next time), and working through a song takes a few minutes. You can drag any word in the timeline if it lands in the wrong place.

    Want the timing in xLights directly? Export timing → Download .xtiming saves the beats, parts and words as an .xtiming file.

Part 2: the lights

  1. Open your show

    Go to 2. Make a sequence and choose your xLights show folder (Your xLights show). The app reads your models, groups, submodels, singing faces and house photo. It remembers the folder for next time.

  2. Line up your house photo

    If the photo doesn't sit under the lights, press Line up photo… and drag it, or use the mouse wheel to size it. This only changes how the preview looks.

  3. Get a starting plan

    Press Suggest a plan. The app picks props for every part, puts steady looks under one or two props that carry the beat, makes loud parts brighter and fuller than quiet ones, and picks a transition into each lift (ramp up, sweep in, burst, colour hit, crossfade, and once per song a build & land). Each part shows how it comes in; open the part and change Comes in with to pick another. With Try 4 ideas, keep the best fit ticked (under Idea settings) it makes four ideas and keeps the one that follows the music best. Don't like it? Press 🎲 Another idea.

    Press Play to watch it on your house. The Fit with the music score shows how well the lights follow the loudness and the beat, and whether loud parts are brighter.

  4. Steer the ideas

    • Feeling (peaceful, joyful, playful, powerful, magical) and Intensity change the kind of effects and how busy they are.
    • Colours for ideas picks the colour scheme; Edit colour schemes… makes your own.
    • Prop mix… uses a prop more, less, always or never, in every part or one kind of part.
    • Prop types… tells the app what a prop is when its name doesn't say ("Big Tree" is a mega tree).
    • Leave out of ideas… keeps props out completely.
    • For: chooses which parts the next idea changes. The 🎲 tick on each part does the same.

    These sit under Ideas → Idea settings in three columns: Look, Props and How ideas are made. Same look for parts with the same name (off to start) gives every Chorus the same effects, every Verse the same, and so on; leave it off for a fresh idea in each part.

  5. Change parts by hand

    Open a part to see its light rows. Each row says which lights, when (the whole time, every beat, drum hits, while someone sings, ...), the effect and the colours. Click a row to change it. Handy tools:

    • 🔁 Loop plays one part over and over while you work on it.
    • 🎲 Randomize gives just that part a new idea.
    • Untick a row to keep it: new ideas fill in around it (it shows a 🔒).
    • Solo / Mute show or hide a row in the preview only.
    • Copy to… copies a part's lights to other parts.
    • 🎨 changes only the colours.
    • 📝 Words on the matrix… shows the sung words on your matrix in the parts you pick (the choruses to start with), so people can sing along. Find the words first (step 4).
    • Click a prop on the house to see what lights it, or drag a box to pick several.
    • View → Only the part that's playing shows just the part you're hearing, opened, and follows the song as it plays: less scrolling while you adjust things.
    • Arrange the boxes (How this works, Ideas, Change what's planned, View): ▾ folds a box to its title. Press 🔒 Layout locked to unlock, then drag a box by its ⠿ grip or use ↑ ↓; press it again to lock. Reset layout puts them back.

    Every change can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

  6. Save your work

    Save project (Ctrl+S) writes a small .lightseq.json file with the beat grid, your parts, the words and the whole light plan. After that, Save writes back to the same file.

    • Auto-save: tick it and every change saves by itself a moment later.
    • Projects ▾ lists your recent projects (and, with Choose projects folder…, every project in that folder). Click one to switch; if you have unsaved changes it asks Save / Don't save / Cancel first. Recent projects also show on the start screen.
    • Opening a project finds its song by itself. The browser asks once per visit before the page may read a file again: press Open on the note at the top, and in Chrome or Edge choose Allow on every visit so it stops asking.

  7. Into xLights

    In Save the sequence, press Save into show folder (or Download .xsq). Open the sequence in xLights, render it, and play. It includes timing tracks for the beats, bars, song parts and words, so you can keep adding effects in xLights the usual way.

Questions and fixes

It keeps asking where the song is.
Choose the song once (or Pick your music folder…); after that the project remembers it. If a note at the top says the browser wants to allow it, press Open and pick Allow on every visit.
Can I use a dark page?
Yes: the menu at the top right switches between Auto (follows your computer), Light and Dark.
I have an idea, or something isn't working.
Tell us on Discord: #suggestions for ideas, #bugs for problems, #help for questions.
Is my music or show uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything runs in your browser. Only the voice and speech models are downloaded (once).
The clicks don't match the drums.
Fix the tempo first (step 2): try ½ tempo or 2× tempo, or Tap tempo along with the song.
xLights says models are missing or "not in your layout".
Make sure xLights has the same show folder open that you chose in the app (the app shows its name at the top of Make a sequence). If the plan was made with another layout, or you renamed props in xLights since, the app shows a ⚠ note listing them: press Fix… to swap each for one of your props, or leave it out. Props that aren't in your show are never written into the saved sequence.
A prop gets odd effects, or isn't used.
Use Prop types… to say what it is, and Prop mix… to use it more.
A prop shows as a single dot in the preview.
That kind of model isn't drawn yet (for example DMX fixtures). The saved sequence still works in xLights. Please report it on GitHub.
"Find words" is slow.
The first run downloads the models. In Chrome or Edge the models use your graphics card; other browsers fall back to the processor, which is slower.
The ideas don't look like my style.
The ideas start from one person's sequencing style (which effects suit which props, learned from 44 finished sequences). Use Feeling, Prop mix and Prop types to steer them, and keep the rows you like.
